How it works

Room to room.

You just pack the boxes. We move everything else.

Before you decide

What would you do otherwise?

Most people ship it home, toss it, or leave it with a friend. Here's what that actually costs.

Ship it home

From about €300 to over €900 round trip, depending on where home is — a rough estimate, not a quote.

Toss it and buy new

Replacing the basics costs about €140–310, and you'll have to set it all up again in September.

Take it on the plane or train

Only what fits in your luggage — a chair, a mini fridge or seven boxes won't.

Leave it with a friend

€0, but it depends on a favour — and on them still being in Madrid in September.

Don't throw it away: we'll keep it and bring it back.

Quick questions

What people ask us most.

How many boxes do I need?

Most students in halls use between 5 and 9. If you're not sure, the calculator estimates it by item, and we always bring one or two extra.

When do I pay?

€20 when you book, deducted from the total. The rest on pickup day, once we see the actual boxes.

What if I change hall or flat in September?

We'll bring it to your new address in Madrid. Just let us know.

Do I have to pack the boxes myself?

Yes, at your own pace, with our material. If you'd rather not, we'll pack them for you for €45.

Can I cancel?

Yes, free up to 14 days before, and we refund the €20.

What happens if something breaks?

Insurance is included for the whole storage period. And if there's ever a problem, you talk directly to us.
