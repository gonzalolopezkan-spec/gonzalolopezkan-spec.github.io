Furniture storage vs self-storage: which is the best option for you?

Updated Jun 17, 2026 · 8 min · By LetsBoxIt

What is the difference between furniture storage and self-storage?

Although many people use these terms interchangeably, furniture storage and self-storage are very different services. The right choice depends on your specific situation.

The key difference: furniture storage is a full professional storage service, while self-storage is a unit where you manage everything yourself.

Furniture storage: a full professional service

What is it?

A service where a specialist company handles:

  • Collecting your items from your home
  • Professional packing
  • Insured transport
  • Storage in a climate-controlled facility
  • Detailed inventory
  • Return whenever you need it

Advantages

  • Door-to-door service: you do nothing. They collect, transport and store your things.
  • Professional packing: your items arrive protected.
  • Maximum security: 24/7 surveillance, alarms, restricted access.
  • Digital inventory: you know exactly where each box is.
  • Ideal for moves: perfect when your new home isn't ready.
  • Protection for valuables: paintings, appliances, antique furniture.

Disadvantages

  • Higher price (€30-150/month vs €25-100 for self-storage)
  • More limited access (by appointment, not 24/7)
  • You wait for staff to assist you

Self-storage: independent storage

What is it?

An individual storage unit where you are responsible for packing, transporting, organising and accessing your things whenever you need to.

Advantages

  • Frequent access: come and go as you like (24/7 in some).
  • Cheaper: €25-100 per month.
  • Flexible space: add or remove items without notice.
  • For temporary use: ideal for seasonal items, tools, sports gear.
  • Lower commitment: many allow short contracts.

Disadvantages

  • Full responsibility on you (packing, transport, organisation)
  • Medium security: more public access, less surveillance
  • No professional protection: risk of damage, theft, pests
  • No inventory and no transport included

When to choose furniture storage

  • A move with a gap between dates: you sell your home but the new one isn't ready.
  • Home renovation: you need to empty the house for works.
  • Long-term storage (6+ months).
  • You have valuable items that need professional packing and surveillance.
  • You don't want logistical stress: you'd rather delegate everything.

When to choose self-storage

  • Frequent access (once or twice a week or more).
  • Limited budget.
  • Short-term storage (1-3 months).
  • Non-fragile items (tools, gear, seasonal things).
  • You can transport your things and want maximum scheduling flexibility.

Real decision cases

Case 1: Ana moves city

Ana sells her flat in Madrid but won't get the Barcelona keys for 3 months. Solution: furniture storage. They collect in Madrid, store it, and deliver in Barcelona. Cost ~€1,500 (transport + 3 months).

Case 2: Carlos renovates his kitchen

Carlos needs to empty the kitchen and living room for 6 weeks. Solution: self-storage. He moves things himself, near-daily access. Cost €80/month ≈ €200 total.

Case 3: María inherits antique furniture

María inherits antique chairs and a 19th-century painting with no space at home. Solution: furniture storage, for specialist packing, 24/7 surveillance and insurance. Cost €80-100/month.

Frequently asked questions

Can I switch from furniture storage to self-storage mid-contract?

Generally yes, though there may be early-termination penalties (1 month).

Does self-storage include insurance?

Usually not in the base rate; it's booked separately (€8-15/month). Furniture storage usually includes basic cover.

Is there a deposit?

Both services typically ask for 1 month as a deposit, returned when you end the contract.
