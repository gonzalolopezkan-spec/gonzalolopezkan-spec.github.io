How much does furniture storage cost in Spain in 2026: a complete price guide

Updated Jun 17, 2026 · 7 min · By LetsBoxIt

How much does furniture storage cost in 2026?

In Spain, furniture storage costs between €30 and €150 per month depending on the size of the space and the city. Madrid and Barcelona are the most expensive, while smaller cities are more competitive.

The average price in the main cities is:

  • Madrid: €5-15 per m³ per month
  • Barcelona: €6-18 per m³ per month
  • Seville: €55-70 for 12 m³ per month
  • Bilbao: similar to Madrid

For a full move of a 3-bedroom flat with 1 month of storage, budget between €900 and €2,500 depending on distance and packing.

Rate types by duration

The cost varies significantly depending on how long you need storage:

  • Daily: €35-75 per day
  • Weekly: €60-200 per week
  • Monthly: €70-300+ per month (the most economical option long term)

If you need storage for more than 2-3 months, many companies offer long-stay discounts that can save you 15-25%.

Prices by space size

  • Very small (1.5-3 m²): €25-50/month
  • Small (3-6 m²): €40-80/month
  • Medium (6-10 m²): €80-150/month
  • Large (10-20 m²): €150-300/month
  • Extra large (20+ m²): €300+/month

Factors that affect the final price

1. Location

The area is crucial. Madrid and Barcelona are 20-30% more expensive than cities like Córdoba or Cádiz.

2. Services included

  • Some providers include transport and packing (more expensive)
  • Others only offer the storage space (cheaper)
  • Premium services with unlimited access cost 10-20% extra

3. Contract length

Committing to 6, 12 or 24 months can reduce the price per m³ by 20-30%.

4. VAT (21%)

Many companies advertise prices without VAT. Always check you are comparing final prices.

5. Access to your items

Unlimited or frequent access can carry a surcharge. Limited access (once a month) is cheaper.

Tips to save money on storage

1. Compare per m³, not per "module"

Companies may quote per "module" or "box", which is imprecise. Always ask for the rate per cubic metre to compare properly.

2. Bundle transport + storage

Negotiate a full package: move + storage can be 15-25% cheaper than booking separately.

3. Use long-stay discounts

If you know you'll need 6 months or more, ask about special rates. Long-term contracts often have 20-30% discounts.

4. Optimise the space

Measure what you really need before booking. Paying for extra space is wasted money.

5. Skip premium services you don't need

24/7 access, climate control or smart-camera surveillance raise the price. Only pay for what you actually need.

Real cost example: Madrid to Barcelona move

  • Removal transport (3 bedrooms): €600-900
  • Storage 10 m² × 2 months: €200-300
  • Approximate total: €800-1,200

With an all-in-one company offering move + storage, you could negotiate €950-1,100 (a 5-15% saving).

Frequently asked questions about prices

Is the price per 30-day period or calendar month?

It is usually per 30-day period or calendar month. Check the exact terms in the contract.

Is there a surcharge for accessing my items?

Some providers charge €15-25 per access. Others include unlimited access. Ask before signing.

Can I change the size of the space?

Generally yes, but there may be moving costs (€50-100). It's better to estimate the initial space well.

What if I cancel the contract early?

Most companies require 15-30 days' notice. Some charge a one-month penalty. Read the fine print.
