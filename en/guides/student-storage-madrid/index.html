Student storage in Madrid 2026: how it works, prices, and what to do for the summer or a full Erasmus year

Updated Sep 14, 2026 · 9 min · By LetsBoxIt

Short answer

In Madrid, students choose between three storage options: self-storage units (€41–200/month depending on size), door-to-door box storage (from €39/box for the whole summer with LetsBoxIt) and full-flat pickup for an Erasmus year. For a summer between two academic years, per-box door-to-door usually wins on price and convenience.

The two cases below come up again and again: a summer between two courses (3–4 months), where you just need a handful of boxes out of the way, and a full Erasmus year (5–12 months), where volume changes what makes sense.

Who this is for

Madrid hosts around 45,181 international students (source: Uniplaces), and Universidad Complutense de Madrid alone, based in Ciudad Universitaria, has over 80,000 students (source: Uniplaces). Add students at IE University, UC3M, Comillas, UAM, UEM, UPM and URJC, and every June the city empties out thousands of rooms at once.

The academic year typically runs from early-to-mid September to around 30 June (see the UAB 2026-27 academic calendar as an example), and residences such as StepHouse and Xior sign contracts that end on 30 June too. That leaves a gap: many international students can't justify paying rent on an empty room over summer, but also can't fly home with everything they own.

That gap is exactly what door-to-door storage is built for: we pick your boxes up before you leave, keep them safe, and bring them back when you're ready to move into your next place.

Two typical cases

Case A: The summer between two courses (3–4 months, ~15–20 boxes)

This is the most common case: your contract at the residence or shared flat ends around 30 June, but your new academic year doesn't start until September. You message us your pickup date and address on WhatsApp, we collect the boxes from your room, store them over summer, and deliver them to your new address when you're back. With 15–20 boxes you'd land right around the 15+ tier at €28/box, and there's no minimum number of months to commit to.

Case B: The full Erasmus year (5–12 months, whole studio contents)

If you're only storing a handful of boxes for the whole year, our per-box pricing still works well. But if you're clearing out an entire studio, furniture included, paying per box for that volume stops making sense. In that case a self-storage unit sized to your belongings, or our full-flat move service, is usually the better option than door-to-door boxes.

Frequently asked questions

How much does student storage cost in Madrid for the summer?

With LetsBoxIt, door-to-door box storage for the whole summer runs from €39 per box (1-4 boxes) down to €28 per box if you store 15 or more. A typical student with 8-10 boxes usually pays €30-33 per box.

Can I store for a full Erasmus year?

Yes, but think about volume first. For a handful of boxes, our per-box rate still works for a long stay. For a whole studio's worth of furniture and belongings, a self-storage unit or our full-flat move service is usually the better fit than paying per box.

What if I only have 3 or 4 boxes?

That's fine, there's no minimum. You pay €39 per box in the 1-4 box tier, and we still handle pickup, storage and delivery.

Do you pick up at my student residence?

Yes. We regularly collect at residences such as StepHouse, Xior, Livensa Alcobendas and colegios mayores, as well as shared flats, across the Comunidad de Madrid.

Is my stuff insured?

Yes, insurance is included in the price you see, with no separate add-on to buy.

When do I need to book by?

We'd recommend booking 1-2 weeks ahead. Pickups in June fill up fast as the academic year ends, so the earlier you message us, the more dates you get to choose from.

Can I change my delivery address?

Yes, just message us on WhatsApp with the new address and date. This is handy if you end up moving to a different flat before we deliver your boxes back.
