Plan B

What would you do otherwise?

For families outside Spain, the real alternative isn't a self-storage unit — it's what you'd have to arrange yourselves, from a distance.

Send it home

From about €300 up to over €900 return, depending on where home is — courier costs add up fast for a whole room's worth of boxes.

Throw it away and buy it again

Replacing the basics can run €140–310, and September starts from zero.

Bring it on the plane or train

Only room for the essentials — a mini-fridge or seven boxes simply won't fit.

Leave it with a friend

€0, but it depends on a favour — and on them still being in Madrid in September.

Don't throw it away — we'll keep it and bring it back.

Questions from families

Insurance included for the whole storage period, with cover of up to €1,500 per customer. If you're storing something worth more, let us know before pickup.

Always us: Gon and Jose do the pickup, the transport and the delivery. Things are stored in a Madrid warehouse we rent for LetsBoxIt.

€20 when you book, by card through Stripe. The rest on pickup day. Every payment comes with its receipt. No monthly fees, no deposit.
