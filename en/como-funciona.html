Before pickup

How to pack a good box.

Nothing complicated. Just a few tips so nothing breaks along the way.

Heavy stuff at the bottom

And in small boxes, so they're easy to lift.

Tape it in an H

Seal it in an H shape on top and bottom, so it holds up on the way.

Fill the gaps

With clothes or paper, so things don't shift inside.

Only what you can lift

Don't take more than you can carry on your own.

Name and room

Write them on two sides of the box, so none get lost.

Wrap anything fragile

In bubble wrap, whenever you can.

Bedding

In bags, inside the box.

What we don't store: vehicles, bikes, food, liquids or hazardous materials.

Frequently asked questions

What people ask us most.

If you've got a question that's not here, message us on WhatsApp and we'll get back to you today.

How long do you store things for?

All summer: from pickup until the end of September. If you need more time, just tell us.

What happens if I'm not there on pickup day?

Ideally you're there, but if you can't be, a flatmate or the concierge can hand them over, as long as you confirm it to us in writing.

How much does an extra box cost on pickup day?

The public rate — the same one you see on the Pricing page.

Are my things insured?

Yes, insurance is included for the whole storage period. If you're storing something valuable, let us know before pickup.

What can't you store?

Vehicles, bikes, food, liquids or hazardous materials.

What if I move flat in September?

No problem, it's quite common. In 2026, almost 1 in 3 customers asked for delivery to a different address than pickup.

Do I have to pack the boxes myself?

Yes, or you can leave it to us for a €45 packing fee.

How many boxes do I need?

Most students in halls use between 5 and 9. If you're not sure, try the calculator.
