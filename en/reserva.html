When do we drop off the boxes?

The boxes are ours (reinforced, they fit perfectly in the van) and so is the tape. We drop them off at your hall or flat a few days before pickup so you have time to pack at your own pace.

If you don't know which date works for you yet, no worries — leave it blank and we'll sort it on WhatsApp after booking. But if you fill it in here, you'd be doing Jose and me a big favour. 🙏

Want us to pack it for you?

By default, the boxes and packing tape are included and we bring them before pickup — you fill them at your own pace.

But if you're swamped (exams, travel, parallel moves), we can do it for you. We arrive on pickup day with all the materials, pack with you and take it away. In under an hour. Zero effort on your part. (We bring music too, to liven things up 🎵)

Cost: €45, added to the total on pickup day (not when you pay the booking fee).
