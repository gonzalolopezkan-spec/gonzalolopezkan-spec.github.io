Price questions

Frequently asked questions

Is the €20 booking fee an extra cost?

No. It's the deposit that blocks your spot this summer, and it's deducted from the total. If your booking comes to €200, you pay €180 on pickup day.

When do I pay the rest?

On pickup day, when we arrive and see the actual boxes you're storing. You don't pay anything before that, and we don't wait until September.

Does the price include VAT?

Yes. Every price on this page already includes it.

What if I bring more boxes than I calculated on pickup day?

It's adjusted using the public rate on this page: you pay for the actual boxes we take, no more, no less.

What happens with boxes I don't use?

You're not charged for them. We always bring one or two extra, just in case.

Is there a discount if I live at StepHouse?

Yes, 15% off storage for StepHouse residents.
