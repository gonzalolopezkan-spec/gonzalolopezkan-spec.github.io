Cookie Policy

Last updated: 30 April 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites download onto the User's device (computer, mobile phone or tablet) when the User browses them. They allow the website to recognise the User, remember their preferences and provide a personalised and improved experience.

This Cookie Policy complies with the provisions of Article 22.2 of Law 34/2002, of 11 July, on Information Society Services and Electronic Commerce (LSSI-CE), with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and with the Guidance on the use of cookies issued by the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) currently in force.

2. Types of cookies

Cookies may be classified according to different criteria:

According to the entity that manages them

  • First-party cookies: these are sent and managed directly by Let's Box It.
  • Third-party cookies: these are sent and managed by entities other than Let's Box It (for example, Google).

According to the retention period

  • Session cookies: these are deleted when the browser is closed.
  • Persistent cookies: these remain stored on the device for a specified period.

According to their purpose

  • Technical cookies (strictly necessary): these enable the basic operation of the Website. They do not require consent.
  • Personalisation cookies: these make it possible to remember the User's preferences.
  • Analytics or measurement cookies: these make it possible to quantify the number of visits and analyse the use of the Website.
  • Behavioural advertising cookies: these make it possible to display personalised advertising.

4. Consent

In accordance with the regulations in force, cookies that are not strictly necessary require the User's prior consent.

When first accessing the Website, an information banner will be displayed where the User may:

  • Accept all cookies: all cookies will be activated, including analytics cookies.
  • Reject non-essential cookies: only the cookies strictly necessary for the operation of the Website will be activated.
  • Configure cookies: individually select which types of cookies you wish to accept.

The User may withdraw consent or change their preferences at any time by accessing the cookie settings via the link available in the footer of the Website.
